The Proof

A Lightning payment is locked to a secret. The recipient signs an invoice that commits to the secret's SHA-256 hash, and their node only reveals the secret, called the preimage, when it takes the money. If you hold the preimage, the invoice was paid. It proves the payment, not who made it: the names on this page are labels.
